About

We Fix the GapsBetween Your Systems

We work with businesses that handle products, orders, stock and supplier files. Our projects improve everyday processes and the tools used to carry them out. Based in Canada.

What we do

We help businesses with the work between their systems: updating supplier prices, passing orders between teams, checking stock and entering records into older software. We work with staff to improve the process and build the tools needed to use it day to day.

Technology is chosen after the operating problem is understood.

How we use AI

We use AI across very different kinds of work — from bulk-updating a 64,000-product catalog to migrating a store’s POS. When we tried a completely new domain, interior visualization, the first client project went from CAD to client-approved renders in two days.

AI is useful for reading documents, preparing code and suggesting matches. We test the output against source files and operating records. Staff review uncertain results and approve business changes.

Checks we build into the work

  • Measure, don’t assume.
  • Every write can be undone.
  • Disable, don’t delete.
  • Automate the repetition. Keep the judgment.
  • The goal is not more software. The goal is a business that runs better.

How we report results

Every number on this site is labeled as measured, an operating estimate, or in progress. If we cannot trace a claim, we do not publish it.
