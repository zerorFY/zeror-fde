Machines do the repeats. People make the calls.

Systems do

  • Collect, move, format and convert data
  • Match, look up and consolidate
  • Repetitive entry and bulk updates
  • Rule execution and verification
  • Drafts, diagnostics and recommendations

People decide

  • Ambiguous cases
  • Business exceptions and approvals
  • Pricing and purchasing decisions
  • Customer-specific situations
  • Final accountability

Depending on the job, we use APIs, scripts, document extraction or a small internal web tool. AI can help read files and suggest matches; it does not approve prices or purchases.
