Tell Us Aboutthe Work

What does your team keep doing by hand? Describe the task, the files and software involved, and the part you would like to improve.

Tell us what takes the time

A short description is enough to start. You can use the points below to explain the work; please leave out passwords and customer details.

  1. The workflow — for example: updating supplier prices, keeping website stock accurate, switching POS, entering data into an old system
  2. The systems and files involved — POS, ERP, website platform, spreadsheets, PDFs
  3. Who touches it — how many people, how often
  4. What goes wrong — what takes too long, breaks, or depends on one person
