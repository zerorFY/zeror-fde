Services

Operational Data Structuring

We turn fragmented business data — PDFs, invoices, Excel files, supplier catalogs, websites and legacy systems — into structured data the business can reuse. Every value keeps its source, and anything ambiguous is flagged for review instead of guessed.

Sources

PDFExcel / CSVInvoicesSupplier documentsProduct catalogsOfficial websitesInternal databasesLegacy systems

Outputs

  • Structured invoice line items with reconciliation totals
  • SKU and model matching
  • Specification enrichment from official sources
  • Price and inventory preparation
  • Exception lists
  • Source references for every value

Proven in

  • Turning supplier documents into structured product data — invoice structuring, model matching and specification enrichment, tested on real data Tested
  • One lookup for prices and inventory — about 40,000 products combined from supplier and internal sources Operating estimate

Questions

What happens when a product match is ambiguous?
It is flagged for human review. We never silently force a near-match.
What if the source data is incomplete?
Missing values are filled from official sources first. If no reliable source exists, the field stays blank and is reported as an exception — it is never guessed.
