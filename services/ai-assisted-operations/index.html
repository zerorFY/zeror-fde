Services

AI-Assisted Operations

We put AI inside real workflows, not only in a chat window. AI helps build systems faster and handle messy data; deterministic scripts run the repeatable work in production; people approve what matters.

Where AI helps

  • Document understanding and data extraction
  • Product-data normalization and controlled matching
  • Source-assisted specification enrichment
  • Exception analysis
  • Preparing and revising code, followed by tests and review

How the work is split

AI can help prepare the tools. Scripts carry out the agreed rules, and a person approves changes to business data.

Proven in

Project status — which tools are in daily use, tested, in progress or designed.

Questions

Does AI make business decisions in these workflows?
No. Pricing, purchasing, approvals, exceptions and final accountability stay with people.
Does AI run your production systems?
Not necessarily. In our website catalog work, AI was used to build the pipeline; the production runs themselves are deterministic scripts, and every write is released by a person.

Beyond operations: the same discipline — real inputs, fixed standards, review loops — also powers our Interior Visualization service.
