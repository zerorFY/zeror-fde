Every case here comes from one working business: a store, a warehouse, a 64,000-product online catalog, dozens of suppliers. Each starts with a problem the team hit every day. Each ends with a result, and says exactly how it was measured.

Case 01online catalog

Measured

Keeping a 64,000-product online catalog accurate

Problem
Supplier prices, images and stock kept drifting out of sync with a 64,000-product website.
What we built
One controlled update pipeline: match, price diagnostic, human approval, bulk write with rollback, read-back check.
Result
386 / 386price updates verified correct after writing

Case 07supplier documents

Tested

Turning supplier documents into structured product data

Problem
Invoices, price lists and spec sheets arrived as PDFs and spreadsheets in every format, and were re-keyed and cross-checked by hand.
What we built
Rule-based extraction and matching: invoice PDF to a fixed-format sheet with a reconciliation total, model matching that flags ambiguity, specs filled from official sources.
Result
44 / 45models matched in a trial against ~6,500 records; 10 ambiguous matches sent to a person
