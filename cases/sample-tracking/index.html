Turning Employee Memory Into Shared Operational Data

Finding whether a showroom sample was installed, and where, used to depend on memory and walking around. We built a shared tracking workflow for 990 samples. About ten people use it; a typical lookup dropped from about 5 minutes to 30 seconds.

The problem

To find whether a showroom sample was installed and where it was located, staff depended heavily on memory and manual searching.

The solution

A shared sample-tracking workflow with:

  • Product records and images
  • Physical location and available locations
  • Installation status
  • Movement and removal
  • Activity history

Results Operating estimate

  • In production, used by about 10 people
  • 990 samples tracked
  • About 8 location lookups per day
  • Typical lookup reduced from about 5 minutes to 30 seconds

Key principle Not every improvement needs more automation. Sometimes the best change is making invisible business state visible and searchable.
