Case

Built From Zero: zeror.ca

We built this business site from an empty domain: registration, DNS, static hosting, bilingual structured content, deployment checks and a publishing system designed for clear human and machine discovery.

What we built

  • Business site — the pages you are reading now, built in English and Chinese from the same structured source
  • Static publishing pipeline — reusable components, metadata, canonical URLs, hreflang, structured data, sitemaps and validation checks generated together
  • Evidence-led case library — measured, estimated, tested and in-progress results are labeled separately instead of blended into marketing claims
  • Discovery surface — pages are organized around real operational questions and linked to the cases that support each capability
  • Measurement — analytics are included on the live domain while local previews stay clean

Why it matters

From an empty domain to a working bilingual business site with a repeatable publishing system — the same end-to-end ownership we bring to operational projects.
