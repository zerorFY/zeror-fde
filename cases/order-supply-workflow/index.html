Case Operating estimate

Reconnecting Sales and Purchasing Information

The same order information was being viewed, re-entered and reorganized again and again across sales and supply-chain work. We rebuilt the flow so it is captured once and passed along. About six people use it in production, removing an estimated 67.7 hours a month of manual work.

The problem

The same order information had to be repeatedly viewed, re-entered and reorganized across sales and supply-chain work.

The rebuilt flow

  1. Order source
  2. Automated capture
  3. Structured operational data
  4. Sales fulfillment decision
  5. Recorded status
  6. Purchasing summary
  7. Supply-chain execution

Results Operating estimate

  • In production, used by about 6 people
  • Sales use it 20+ times per operating day
  • Supply-chain summary about once per day
  • About 67.7 hours per month of active manual work removed

The time figure is an operating estimate based on confirmed workflow frequency and task-time assumptions, not backend time tracking.

Key principle Automation moves information. People keep the business decisions.
