Interior Visualization

Turn Your CAD and Design Concept Into Client-Ready Interior Visuals

For interior designers, renovation companies, and furniture and lighting stores that already have a design — and need visuals a client can understand and approve. Built on real drawings and dimensions, with one visual standard across every view, revised until it is right.

What you send → what you get

You sendYou get
CAD floor plans and elevationsFull-room concept renders
Furniture layout and room dimensionsMultiple viewpoints of the same space
Style, material and color referencesClose-ups of key areas
Furniture and lighting product images, with sizesSide-by-side options to compare

The design is yours. We make it visible.

Built on real dimensions, not guesswork

With CAD, room sizes and product sizes, proportions in the render follow the real space and the real products — not what an image generator imagines.

One visual standard per project

Every render in a project follows the same rules: color temperature, white balance, wall and floor colors, materials, furniture style, logo and brand elements.

Example from a real project Warm neutral beige, never yellow, orange or gold; lighting kept around 3000K warm white — so every view looks like the same real space.

Revise until it is right

Process Render → Review → Modify → Final

What can change between rounds:

  • Furniture — swap, move, rotate, resize
  • Materials — velvet, wood, stone; wall and rug colors
  • Lighting — fixtures, fixture size, color temperature, white balance
  • Branding — logo, signage, wall graphics
  • Décor — add or remove decorative elements; try different styles

Compare options before deciding

A or B feature wall. This sofa or that one. Two lighting schemes. Compare one area quickly, without redoing the whole render.

Show a product in a real space

Replace a pendant, sconce, sofa, chair, table, cabinet, rug or artwork in an existing photo or render — scaled to the product’s real size when dimensions are provided. Useful for furniture and lighting stores helping customers choose.

First client project

A commercial storefront interior:

  • Full space built from the client’s CAD and design
  • Multiple viewpoints
  • Furniture, material, logo and wall-design changes
  • One color-temperature standard across all views
  • Several revision rounds

The client approved the final renders.

What this is — and what it is not

These are concept visuals for communication, approval and sales. They are not construction documents.

Space planning, layout and the core design come from the designer. Structural design, MEP, code compliance and permit drawings still need a licensed designer, architect or engineer.

How we work with AI

The renders are AI-assisted. What makes them usable is the discipline around the AI: real drawings and dimensions as input, one fixed visual standard per project, and human review at every round.

Turnaround and pricing are quoted per project.
